## Entry text

The self-checkout has ONE job, and it screams at me for doing mine: "Unexpected item in the bagging area!" It's my bag. I brought it from home, on purpose. And the item is expected — I scanned it while its camera watched me scan it. Then the light turns red, my milk is held hostage, and a human is summoned to judge me like I hid a TV inside a banana.
